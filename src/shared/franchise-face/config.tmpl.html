Pick the images in rotation and style the overlay. The face changes image on the interval below, and on a wrist shake if enabled.

Rotation

Clock & overlay

Tap a position button, then tap a spot on the watch. Items on the same spot sit side by side.

Temperature and the weather icon use your phone's approximate location to fetch a forecast, only while one of them is on the watch. Weather data by Open-Meteo.com (CC BY 4.0).

Images

Tap to add or remove an image from the rotation. Images marked bundled are stored on the watch and show even without your phone; the rest stream from the phone when shown. The ✎ button on a selected image sets a look just for it.
